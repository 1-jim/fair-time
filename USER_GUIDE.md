# Fair Time: coach's guide

**Fair game time for every player, even when match day doesn't go to plan.**

Fair Time runs your substitutions on match day. You load the Spond attendee list and pick a substitution plan. The app then tells you who comes off and who goes on, keeps track of everyone's minutes, and produces a summary at the end.

When someone doesn't turn up, gets injured or arrives late, you tap their name and the rotation adjusts itself. There's no spreadsheet to redo on the touchline.

> This is an early version built by a club parent. Please try it and tell us what works and what doesn't: see [Feedback](#feedback) at the end.

**Open the app:** `https://1-jim.github.io/fair-time/`

## 1. Before match day

### Install it on your phone

Open the link above, then:

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the menu, then **Install app**.

Open it once with signal so it's saved on your phone. After that it works with no signal at the ground.

**iPhone users:** always open Fair Time from the home-screen icon. iPhones keep the home-screen app's data separate from Safari, so a squad loaded in one won't appear in the other.

### Get the squad from Spond

1. Open the event in Spond.
2. Tap the number of people going, then the **Excel** icon.
3. Get the file into Fair Time:
   - **Android:** choose **Fair Time** from the share menu. The squad loads straight in.
   - **iPhone:** save the file to Files. Open Fair Time and tap **Import Spond file**.

Fair Time reads names, responses and the group name. Contact details, addresses and dates of birth in the Spond file are ignored and never stored. The group name is how player notes stay with the same child each week. Importing a Spond file replaces the squad already on this phone: names from an earlier event are removed, and the teams are cleared so you can set them up for this event.

No Spond file? Tap **Paste names** and paste a list, one name per line. You can also paste a **Share team list** message from WhatsApp. If this phone already has a squad, Fair Time asks you to confirm, then replaces that list. The event title and date stay. The teams are cleared so you can set them up again.

### Check the squad

Everyone appears on the **Squad** screen, marked as one of:

| Mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Going** | Said yes in Spond |
| **Late** | Didn't answer, or might be late. They start as "not arrived" and join in when you tap them. |
| **Out** | Can't make it. Taken off their team. Everyone else stays where you put them. |

Tap to change anyone's mark. That does not reshuffle the teams. The list stays in alphabetical order, so names never jump around when you change them.

Use the **Going**, **Late or unsure** and **Not going** buttons above the list to filter it. Tap one to show only those players, or tap more than one to combine them (for example Going and Late together). The numbers on the buttons update as you change marks. Tap **Show all** to clear the filter.

Add a missing player at the bottom.

The **Event title** at the top is included when you send the squad on. Spond fills it in. Change it if you need to, for example "U12 home".

### Give another coach the same squad

Each phone keeps its own copy. To hand the squad and teams to another coach:

1. On **Squad** or **Setup**, tap **Export squad file**.
2. Send the file by WhatsApp, email or AirDrop.
3. On the other phone:
   - **Android, Fair Time saved on the home screen:** open the file in WhatsApp, tap Share, then **Fair Time**. The app warns you first, then replaces whatever is already on that phone, including a game that has started.
   - **iPhone:** save the file to Files, open Fair Time and tap **Import squad file** (it stays in the menu during a match). The app warns you first, then replaces whatever is already on that phone, including a game that has started. An iPhone does not list a home-screen app in the WhatsApp share sheet.

Export from **Squad** or **Setup**: both send the same file. They get the same names, who's going or late, the teams and the settings. Importing puts those teams back as they were, and opening Setup does not reshuffle them. It does not copy minutes from a game that has already started. For a full match-day backup, use **Menu > Back up this session**.

Set the teams up before you export if you want your own split included. Until then the file has the squad and the settings, and the teams are filled in when you open Setup.

## 2. Setting up teams

On the **Setup** screen:

| Setting | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Teams | 2 |
| Players on the pitch | 9 |
| Minutes per game | 16 |
| Games per team | 3 |
| Halves per game | 2 |
| Full reshuffle at half-time and between games | On (recommended) |

### Choosing a substitution plan

Fair Time tries each plan against a whole match day before you pick, and shows how each one would turn out:

| Column | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Fairness gap** | The difference between the most and least time played across the day, if nobody drops out. Smaller is fairer. |
| **Stoppages** | How many times you'll be subbing during play. Half-time and between games don't count. |
| **Longest rest** | The longest anyone sits on the bench in one go. |

The plan marked **Best** balances fairness against disruption. For example, with 13 players and 9 on the pitch, "Swap 4 every 5 min" gives about a 3-minute gap with 6 stoppages. "Swap 4 every 2 min" gets the gap down to 2 minutes, but means 17 stoppages.

### Splitting the squad

Start with **Split A to Z** or **Shuffle** to get a rough split, then fine-tune by hand. This is where you balance the teams by ability and experience, or set up an A and a B team. There are no player ratings: you move people where you want them.

| To do this | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Move a player to another team | Drag their name onto the other team. On a phone, press and hold the name for a moment, then drag. |
| Swap two players | Drag one name onto a player in the other team. Both swap over in one go. |
| Do it without dragging | Tap a name to select it (it gets a blue outline), then tap **Move here** on another team, or tap a player in another team to swap them. |
| Change your mind | Tap **Undo**. It goes back one move at a time, and also undoes a re-split. |
| Name the teams | Tap the team name and type, e.g. "A team" and "B team". |

Each team shows how many players it has, how many have confirmed, and the bench size, so you can see the balance as you go. Names stay in alphabetical order within each team.

When the teams look right, tap **Lock teams and start**. Teams are fixed from then on, apart from moving a player between teams before a kick-off or at full-time on the Match screen.

**Share team list** (on the Match screen, and in the menu) sends the event title, each team and the plan as a message for WhatsApp or anywhere else. Another coach can paste that message into **Paste names**. That replaces their squad list and keeps their event title and date. It does not bring across the teams or the settings. Use **Export squad file** when the other coach needs those too.

## 3. On the touchline

### The Match screen

The clock is at the top, with a countdown to the next change. Below it, every player is shown as a coloured card:

| Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 🟩 Green | On the pitch |
| 🟥 Red | On the pitch, coming off next |
| 🟨 Amber | On the bench, going on next |
| 🟦 Blue | On the bench |
| ⬜ Grey | Not available (injured, gone home, not arrived) |

Each card shows the minutes played so far. It also shows how far that player is ahead (+) or behind (-) their fair share. The **Next change** list reads left to right: the player coming on, then the player they are coming on for.

With two teams on one phone, use the tabs at the top. A red dot on a tab means that team has a change due.

### Running a game

1. Tap **Kick off**.
2. When a change is due, the phone beeps and a full-screen alert shows the pairs. The player **coming on** is on the left. The player they are coming on for is on the right.
3. Make the swaps, then tap **Done**.
   - Ball not dead yet? Tap **Wait 30 sec**.
   - Want to keep a pair on? Tap that pair to leave it out.
   - Not the right moment at all? Tap **Skip this change**.
4. Stoppage before the change is due? Tap **Sub now** to do it early.
5. The clock shows **Elapsed** on the left and **Overtime** on the right. Overtime is how far this half has run past its length, from stoppages and delays. The board turns red, and the phone beeps every 30 seconds.
6. Tap **Half-time** or **Full-time** when the referee blows. If a half runs 25% over its length with no tap, Fair Time ends it and records that nobody tapped.
7. At half-time and between games, Fair Time suggests a bigger reshuffle to even things out. Check it, then tap **Done**.

**Pause** stops the clock for a long stoppage, so that time is not added to overtime. **Undo** reverses your last action if you tap the wrong thing. Undoing an automatic end leaves the half running, so you can tap **Half-time** or **Full-time** yourself.

The screen stays on while a game is running. Keep the sound on: iPhones don't vibrate for web apps.

## 4. When things change

Tap any player's card for options.

| Situation | What to tap | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Injured | **Injured** | They come off and the bench player furthest behind comes on straight away. |
| Fit again | **Fit to play again** | They go to the bench and are prioritised, as they're now behind. |
| Head knock | **Suspected concussion** | They're out for the rest of the day and can't be put back by accident. |
| Arrives late | **Arrived** | They join the bench and start earning fair time from that moment. |
| Goes home early | **Gone home** | Removed from the rotation. |
| Opposition short | **Lend to the opposition** | Their time still counts as playing. |
| Manual swap | Pick a name under **Swap off for** or **Bring on for** | Swaps just those two. |
| Move between teams | **Move to** (before kick-off or at full-time) | Moves them with their minutes. |
| Not on the list at all | **Add player** at the bottom | Adds them to the bench. |
| Something to work on | The green or red side of a split under **Notes**, such as **Low contact** | Counts a good execution, or an error, for that player and this event. |

To change the plan mid-game, for example in heavy rain or with more players than expected, tap **Sub plan**.

### How fairness works

Fair Time doesn't follow a fixed rota, which is why it copes when plans fall apart. It shares the pitch time evenly among whoever is available at each moment. At every change, it brings on the bench players furthest behind and takes off those furthest ahead.

So a player who arrives in game 2 isn't "owed" game 1. An injured player isn't owed the time they were off either.

## 5. After the games

At full-time, each game gets a quick recap. After the last game, open **Summary** for the whole day:

- A plain-English list of what happened, for example:
  - "Josh was injured 4:06 into game 1 and didn't return."
  - "Sam and Alfie got 1 minute more than their fair share."
- A table of minutes for every player in every game, with anyone outside the threshold highlighted.

Buttons at the top of the Summary:

- **Share summary** sends the text to WhatsApp, email or anywhere else.
- **Export CSV** gives you the minutes as a spreadsheet.
- **Backup (JSON)** saves the whole session. Use **Menu > Restore a backup** to load it on another phone.

### Starting next week

**Menu > Start a new session** clears the squad, the teams and the minutes. It warns you first and offers a backup. Player notes are kept.

## 6. Player notes

Tap a player's name on the Squad screen, or open their card on the Match screen. Under **Notes**, each skill is a split. Tap the green side when it is done well, or the red side for the error. Each tap counts for this event, so you can see the balance on the row, for example **+2 -1**.

**Summary** opens as soon as the squad has names, before any match. It lists the skill feedback for this event, for the squad and for each player. **Share skill feedback** sends that text to WhatsApp or anywhere else. You do not need to set up teams.

**Defence** is the collision and the line.

- **Tackle height**: **Low contact** below the sternum, or **Upright / high**.
- **Tracking**: **Hips boxed**, feet square and the angle cut off, or **Ankles broken**.
- **Line speed**: **Squeeze**, the line up together, or **Dog leg**, one player shot out or left behind.

**Attack** is shape and moving the ball.

- **Fix & pass**: **Committed**, the defender was drawn first, or **Shovelled**, the pass went early.
- **Ball catch**: **Hands early**, or **Body catch**.
- **Support line**: **Deception**, a real running line, or **Flat / static**.

**Contest** is the breakdown and the set piece.

- **Ruck arrival**: **Clean over**, or **Flopped**.
- **Body shape**: **Tower**, a flat back, or **Hinged** from the waist.
- **Ball presentation**: **Long placement** away from the contest, or **Sloppy release**.

Under **TREDS**, tap a positive behaviour: teamwork, respect, enjoyment, discipline or sportsmanship. The TREDS box is separate, for example "helped a teammate up".

Tap **Set coach name** on the Squad screen. Every note is marked with that name, so the same point from two coaches shows as reaffirmed. If the name is not set yet, the first note asks for it.

Notes stay on this phone when you start a new session or import next week's Spond file. The same spelling of a name, in the same Spond group, keeps the same notes. Two age groups on one phone stay separate because the group name from the file is part of the match.

On the squad list, a result with a number means that many taps, or that many coaches when more than one coach saw it. **Low contact · 2** has been tapped twice, or seen by two coaches.

### Sharing notes between coaches

**Menu > Player notes > Share notes file** sends a file, which you can pass on in WhatsApp. Another coach taps **Import notes file** on their phone. Importing the same file again does not add a second copy.

When two coaches note the same topic for the same player, it shows as reaffirmed, with both names. The same coach seeing it again at a later session stays as one coach, and both sessions are listed.

The match summary and the minutes file do not include these notes. Share the notes file with the coaches who should read them.

You can remove a note you wrote. Another coach's note stays until they remove it on their own phone.

## 7. Good to know

- **One phone per team** works best if coaches are on different pitches. Export the squad file to the other phone before you lock in, then each phone runs its own game. Minutes are not shared live between phones.
- **Everything stays on your phone.** Nothing is sent to a server, and no player data is stored online.
- **No signal at the ground is fine**, as long as you've opened the app once beforehand.
- **Reloading or locking the phone is safe.** The clock keeps correct time and nothing is lost.
- **Changing the "flag anyone this far from fair share" setting** (in Setup) controls what the summary highlights. The default is 1 minute.

### Not in this version yet

- Position rules, such as always keeping enough scrum-trained players on.
- Tracking fairness across a whole season rather than one day.
- Live sync between two coaches' phones.
- Pulling the squad directly from Spond, rather than via the Excel file.

1. **Did it keep game time fair?** Did the summary match how the day actually felt?
2. **Was it usable on the touchline?** Was anything hard to read, too small, or too slow in the moment?
